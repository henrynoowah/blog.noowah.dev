description: "A movement is a system you can hold — and it either keeps time or it does not."

---

Software hides. You can ship something that mostly works, and nobody sees the seam
until it fails in production six weeks later.

A movement doesn't let you do that. Hundreds of parts, no abstraction layer, and
it either keeps time or it does not. Nothing has taught me more about finishing a
job properly.

More on modding — and on what breaks when you rush it — soon.
